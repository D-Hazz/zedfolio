Overview

CoFiLab focuses on enabling small-scale funding and task-based collaboration with a lighter, more transparent economic model. The platform is designed around impactful UX and a simplified path from contribution to fulfillment.

It is a strong example of the kind of product thinking I apply: reduce friction, clarify the user journey, and make secure digital payments feel approachable.
