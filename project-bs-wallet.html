BS Wallet

A multi-chain wallet experience focused on usability, trust, and cross-network transaction clarity.

Overview

BS Wallet is a wallet product concept designed to simplify access to multiple blockchain and payment environments through a cleaner user experience. The goal is to make crypto transactions more understandable without compromising speed or security.

The product combines frontend UX logic, wallet flow design, and multi-network relationships into one coherent digital experience.

  • Cross-chain asset visibility
  • Lightning and BTC interaction flows
  • Simple onboarding and portfolio experience

Project snapshot

Type: Wallet product and mobile UX Stack: React Native, wallet flows, interfaces Focus: Simplicity, trust, and multi-network operations
